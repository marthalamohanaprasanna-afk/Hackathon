MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)
Contract Reference: MSA-NEX-2024-01

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of January 1, 2024 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

CLIENT:
Nexus Global Logistics Inc., a Washington corporation having its principal place of business at 450 Harbor Boulevard, Seattle, WA 98101 ("Nexus" or "Client"), and

PROVIDER:
Acme Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 100 Silicon Way, San Jose, CA 95112 ("Acme" or "Provider").

1. ENGAGEMENT AND STATEMENTS OF WORK (SOW)
Provider shall perform the technology architecture, cloud migration, and infrastructure management services specified in Statements of Work and Purchase Orders issued pursuant to this Agreement.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL OBLIGATIONS
2.1 Initial Term: The initial term of this Agreement commences on the Effective Date and expires on December 31, 2025 (a 24-month initial term).
2.2 Automatic Renewal & Deadline Notice: This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. The milestone notice deadline for non-renewal is November 1, 2025.

3. FEES, PAYMENT TERMS, AND SPEND LIMITS
3.1 Invoices: Provider shall invoice Client upon completion of deliverables. All invoices must be payable within thirty (30) days ("Net 30") of receipt.
3.2 Purchase Order Cap: Invoices issued without a corresponding authorized Purchase Order, or exceeding the approved PO ceiling amount, shall be subject to immediate audit and approval withholding.

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & UPTIME
Provider guarantees a monthly uptime of 99.9% for cloud hosting and critical routing APIs. Any outage exceeding four (4) consecutive hours requires a 10% service credit against the following month's fees.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA SECURITY
Both parties agree that all proprietary business data, customer shipping manifests, and technical architecture designs are confidential and shall be protected for a period of five (5) years following termination of this Agreement.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
